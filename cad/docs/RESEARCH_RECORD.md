the publications portal returned a loading/CSS-error page. Neither supplied an

complete AE300 STEP assembly. This is a search result, not proof that no OEM CAD
exists. No third-party generic diesel model was relabelled as AE300.

Configuration-dependent parts cannot be certified complete without the selected
E4 configuration/serial baseline and applicable IPC. The later manifold-fed BPA
arrangement shown in the uploaded maintenance manual is used as a topology
reference; that does not establish applicability to a particular engine.

To replace reconstruction assumptions, obtain OEM-authorized geometry/drawings,
casting and gallery sections, sensor/connector supplier drawings, cam/rod/gear
definitions, and the applicable harness wire list. ECU PCB/firmware is a separate
unavailable design input. These were not invented or reverse-engineered from
undocumented electrical interfaces.
